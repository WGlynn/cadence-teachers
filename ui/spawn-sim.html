teaching market simulation: brains teach brains, pay follows measured gain

Each circle is a Cadence brain. Arrows are teaching sessions this round (teacher to student). Green = how much of the true task the brain knows. Wallet = total pay earned (teaching + pedigree share).
This round's sessions
Honest scope: small Cadence brains on a 32-situation task, 8 brains, 12 rounds. Pay = the student's measured gain on the true task (a twin-style before/after test); when a brain re-teaches something it learned from another brain, a 30% share goes back to the original source. Partner choice is learned from pay in the CHOOSE runs, random in PEER runs. What to teach is still random within what the teacher knows.

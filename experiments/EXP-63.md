## Summary
- Question: EXP-57's rule (a decision whose next observation is unchanged never enters Cadence's temporal context, the key of its episodic records) took clean greedy passes of the hidden-progress chain from 4/96 to 38/96, did nothing where the inventory is visible (EXP-60), and broke where a correct action is invisible (EXP-59: stage-2 first try -.157, clean passes 9 vs 2 of 96), because "the observation did not change" is only a proxy for "the decision did nothing". Upstream #202 (Bernhard, 2026-10-15) proposes the observation rule as an opt-in gene and asks for the invisible-action boundary to be measured. Does a gate keyed on the outcome (hold only decisions the world answered as failures) keep the gain and remove the break?
- Setup: EXP-56 exactly (0.83 `Brain.compose(7, 5, modules=(48, 24), arousal=True, memory_choice=True)`, efference off, V5 chamber rules, 10 decisions per attempt, natural and corrective signals, readings in sequence on a saved copy). Arms: `ctx5` (the founder key, memory_context .5), `hold5` (EXP-57's observation gate), `out5` (outcome gate: before the next call, a decision the chamber answered as a failure is taken back out of the context; a correct decision always stays in, visible or not; never across done). The outcome is the chamber's own success flag for the decision: Cadence's `step()` does not receive it, and in this chamber neither the observation nor the per-step reward (0 for a miss and for an invisible success alike under the natural signal) can tell a miss from the invisible WOOD.
  - Invisible chain (EXP-59's A, A, B, C): ctx5 / hold5 / out5, 48 fresh seeds per signal (63_000 + i), horizon fixed by the pilot (below).
  - Hidden chain (EXP-56/57's A, B, A, B): ctx5 / out5, 24 fresh seeds per signal (63_000 + i), 120 attempts.
  - Visible chain (EXP-60's inventory): ctx5 / out5, 24 fresh seeds per signal (63_000 + i), 120 attempts.
  - On the hidden and visible chains a wrong action is the only decision that leaves the observation unchanged, so out5 holds exactly the decisions hold5 holds; there out5 is a fresh-seed replication of hold5 at the new head, not a new mechanism. The two gates differ only on the invisible chain.
